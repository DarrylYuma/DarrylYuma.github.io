HR Analytics Dashboard

PROJECT OVERVIEW

This project explores the factors influencing employee attrition, compensation, and workforce trends using an HR dataset containing 1,470 employee records. Through SQL analysis and an interactive Power BI dashboard, the project investigates workforce demographics, career progression, compensation fairness, employee benefits, and salary growth to uncover actionable business insights.

  • Employee attrition across departments and demographics.
  • Career progression and promotion stagnation.
  • Compensation fairness by gender and job role.
  • Workplace benefits and their relationship with retention.
  • Salary progression across job roles and tenure.

KEY FINDINGS

Employee Attrition

  • Sales recorded the highest attrition rate at 20.63%, while Research & Development maintained the lowest.
  • Employees under 35 years old experienced more than double the attrition rate of older employees.
  • Employees with five years of service or less were significantly more likely to leave the organization.

Career Progression

  • Promotion stagnation became increasingly associated with attrition among long-tenured employees.
  • Employees with more than five years of service but limited promotion opportunities exhibited elevated attrition rates.

Compensation & Benefits

  • Salary positioning was generally balanced across genders within the same job role, with notable exceptions among Managers and Research Directors.
  • Lower stock option levels showed a stronger relationship with attrition than salary hike percentage.

Salary Growth

  • Monthly income generally increased with tenure across most job roles.
  • Research Directors were the only notable exception, showing limited salary differentiation between tenure groups.

RECOMMENDATIONS

  • Strengthen onboarding, mentoring, and career development programs for early-career employees.
  • Establish clearer promotion pathways for long-tenured employees to reduce career stagnation.
  • Evaluate employee benefit programs, particularly stock option offerings, as part of retention initiatives.
  • Continue monitoring compensation fairness across job roles where larger differences were observed.

SQL ANALYSIS

Eight SQL analyses were developed to answer workforce-related business questions covering employee attrition, career stagnation, compensation fairness, employee benefits, and salary progression. Techniques used include CTEs, window functions, CASE expressions, aggregation, and median calculations to transform HR data into actionable business insights.
